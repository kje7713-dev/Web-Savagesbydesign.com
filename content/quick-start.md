<h1>Quick Start Guide</h1>

<p><strong>Get up and running in 10 minutes.</strong></p>

<p>This guide walks you through creating your first training block and running your first workout. Skip the theory. Just the steps you need to start tracking.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Step 1: Launch the App</h2>

<p>Open Savage By Design. You'll see four main buttons on the home screen:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>BLOCKS</strong> – Where you create and manage training programs</li>
<li><strong>BLOCK HISTORY</strong> – Your archived workouts</li>
<li><strong>DATA MANAGEMENT</strong> – Backup and restore</li>
<li><strong>GO PRO</strong> – Subscription features</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Tap BLOCKS to get started.</strong></p>

<hr>

<h2>Step 2: Create Your First Block</h2>

<p>On the Blocks screen, tap <strong>CREATE NEW BLOCK</strong> at the bottom.</p>

<h3>Set Block Details</h3>

<ol>
<li><strong>Name</strong>: Give it a name like "4-Week Strength Program"</li>
<li><strong>Number of Weeks</strong>: Start with 4 weeks</li>
<li><strong>Progression Type</strong>: Choose "Weight" if you want to add weight each week, or "Volume" to add sets</li>
<li><strong>Delta Weight</strong>: Enter 5 (adds 5 lbs per week to exercises)</li>
</ol>

<hr>

<h2>Step 3: Create Your First Day</h2>

<p>You'll see tabs at the top: Day 1, Day 2, etc.</p>

<p>Tap <strong>Day 1</strong> and name it something like "Upper Body".</p>

<h3>Add Exercises</h3>

<p>Tap <strong>+ ADD EXERCISE</strong> to add your first exercise.</p>

<p><strong>Example: Bench Press</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Name</strong>: Bench Press</li>
<li><strong>Type</strong>: Strength</li>
<li><strong>Sets</strong>: 4</li>
<li><strong>Reps</strong>: 8</li>
<li><strong>Weight</strong>: 135 (your starting weight in lbs)</li>
<li><strong>Rest</strong>: 120 (seconds between sets)</li>
</ul>

<p>Tap <strong>Save</strong> to add the exercise.</p>

<p><strong>Add 2-3 more exercises</strong> using the same process:</p>
<ul>
<li>Overhead Press: 3 sets x 10 reps</li>
<li>Barbell Rows: 4 sets x 8 reps</li>
<li>Pull-ups: 3 sets x 6 reps</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>Step 4: Add More Days (Optional)</h2>

<p>Tap <strong>Day 2</strong> and repeat the process. Name it "Lower Body" and add exercises like:</p>

<ul>
<li>Back Squat: 4 sets x 8 reps</li>
<li>Romanian Deadlift: 3 sets x 10 reps</li>
<li>Leg Press: 3 sets x 12 reps</li>
</ul>

<p>For your first block, 2-3 days is plenty.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Step 5: Save Your Block</h2>

<p>Once you've added all your days and exercises, tap <strong>SAVE</strong> in the top-right corner.</p>

<p>Your block is now created and ready to run!</p>

<hr>

<h2>Step 6: Run Your First Workout</h2>

<p>Back on the Blocks screen, you'll see your newly created block.</p>

<p>Tap <strong>RUN</strong> to start your first workout session.</p>

<h3>Navigate the Workout</h3>

<ul>
<li><strong>Week selector</strong> at the top shows which week you're on (Week 1)</li>
<li><strong>Day tabs</strong> let you switch between workout days</li>
<li><strong>Exercise list</strong> shows all your exercises for today</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>Step 7: Log Your Sets</h2>

<p>For each exercise, you'll see the planned sets.</p>

<p><strong>To log a set:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Tap on the set to expand it</li>
<li>Adjust the weight, reps, or other values if needed using the <strong>+ / -</strong> buttons</li>
<li>Check the <strong>✓</strong> box when you complete the set</li>
<li>The set turns green to show it's logged</li>
</ol>

<p>The app auto-saves as you go. You can close it anytime and come back later.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Step 8: Finish Your Workout</h2>

<p>When you're done:</p>

<ol>
<li>Tap <strong>Close Session</strong> in the top-left</li>
<li>Your workout is saved automatically</li>
<li>All data goes into your Block History</li>
</ol>

<hr>

<h2>Step 9: Progress to Next Week</h2>

<p>When you complete all days in Week 1, the app shows a completion screen.</p>

<p>Tap <strong>Continue to Next Week</strong> to move to Week 2.</p>

<p><strong>Progression happens automatically:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>If you set Delta Weight to 5 lbs, all your exercises now show 5 lbs more</li>
<li>If you set Delta Sets, you'll see an extra set added</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>Step 10: Review Your History</h2>

<p>From the home screen, tap <strong>BLOCK HISTORY</strong>.</p>

<p>You'll see all completed blocks. Tap <strong>REVIEW</strong> on any block to see:</p>

<ul>
<li>Every set you logged</li>
<li>Weight progression over weeks</li>
<li>Completion dates</li>
<li>Notes you added</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>Pro Tips for Getting Started</h2>

<ul>
<li><strong>Start simple</strong>: 3-4 days per week, 4-5 exercises per day</li>
<li><strong>Be realistic</strong>: Use weights you can complete with good form</li>
<li><strong>Log everything</strong>: Even if you don't hit your target, log what you did</li>
<li><strong>Backup your data</strong>: Go to DATA MANAGEMENT → EXPORT ALL DATA monthly</li>
<li><strong>Check out Pro</strong>: Whiteboard view is great for visualizing your week</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>Common First-Time Questions</h2>

<p><strong>Q: Can I edit a block after I create it?</strong></p>
<p>A: Yes. From the Blocks screen, tap the block, then tap Edit. Make your changes and save.</p>

<p><strong>Q: What if I miss a workout?</strong></p>
<p>A: No problem. The app doesn't force completion. Skip the day and come back whenever you can.</p>

<p><strong>Q: Can I add exercises mid-block?</strong></p>
<p>A: It's better to edit the block template before running it. Once running, the structure is set for that cycle.</p>

<p><strong>Q: How do I delete a block?</strong></p>
<p>A: Swipe left on the block in the Blocks screen, then tap Delete.</p>

<p><strong>Q: What's the difference between Blocks and Block History?</strong></p>
<p>A: BLOCKS shows active training programs. BLOCK HISTORY shows completed/archived programs.</p>
