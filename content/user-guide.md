<h2 id="understanding-training-blocks">Understanding Training Blocks</h2>

<h3>What is a Training Block?</h3>

<p>A <strong>training block</strong> is a structured workout program that spans multiple weeks. Think of it as a complete training cycle designed to help you achieve specific fitness goals.</p>

<p><strong>Key Components:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Block Name</strong> – What you call your program (e.g., "Summer Strength Program")</li>
<li><strong>Number of Weeks</strong> – How long the block runs (typically 4-12 weeks)</li>
<li><strong>Days</strong> – Individual workout days that repeat each week</li>
<li><strong>Exercises</strong> – The specific movements you'll perform</li>
</ul>

<h3>Block Periodization</h3>

<p>The app uses <strong>block periodization</strong>, a proven training method where:</p>
<ul>
<li>You plan workouts in advance</li>
<li>Exercises progress week-over-week (progressive overload)</li>
<li>You can program deload weeks for recovery</li>
<li>Each week builds on the previous one</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Example Block Structure:</strong></p>
<pre><code>Block: "Spring Strength Cycle"
├── Week 1-3: Build phase
├── Week 4: Deload
└── Weeks 5-6: Peak phase

Days per week:
├── Day 1: Upper Body Push
├── Day 2: Lower Body
├── Day 3: Upper Body Pull
└── Day 4: Conditioning
</code></pre>

<h3>Training Goals</h3>

<p>Blocks can target different goals:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Strength</strong> – Build maximum force production</li>
<li><strong>Hypertrophy</strong> – Muscle size and growth</li>
<li><strong>Power</strong> – Explosive strength</li>
<li><strong>Conditioning</strong> – Cardiovascular fitness</li>
<li><strong>Mixed</strong> – Combination of goals</li>
<li><strong>Peaking</strong> – Preparing for competition</li>
<li><strong>Deload</strong> – Recovery and adaptation</li>
<li><strong>Rehab</strong> – Injury recovery</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="creating-your-first-block">Creating Your First Block</h2>

<h3>Step 1: Access Block Builder</h3>

<ol>
<li>From the Home Screen, tap <strong>BLOCKS</strong></li>
<li>Tap <strong>CREATE NEW BLOCK</strong> at the bottom</li>
<li>You'll enter the Block Builder interface</li>
</ol>

<h3>Step 2: Set Block Details</h3>

<p><strong>Block Information:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Name</strong> – Give your block a memorable name (e.g., "12-Week Strength Builder")</li>
<li><strong>Description</strong> (optional) – Add notes about the program</li>
<li><strong>Number of Weeks</strong> – Choose how many weeks (1-52)</li>
<li><strong>Progression Type</strong> – How exercises will progress:
<ul>
<li><strong>Weight</strong> – Add weight each week</li>
<li><strong>Volume</strong> – Add sets/reps each week</li>
<li><strong>Custom</strong> – Manual progression</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Progression Settings:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Delta Weight</strong> – How much weight to add per week (e.g., 5 lbs)</li>
<li><strong>Delta Sets</strong> – How many additional sets per week (e.g., 1 set)</li>
</ul>

<h3>Step 3: Create Day Templates</h3>

<p>Each block contains <strong>day templates</strong> that define your workouts:</p>

<ol>
<li><strong>Select a Day Tab</strong> at the top (Day 1, Day 2, etc.)</li>
<li><strong>Name Your Day</strong> – E.g., "Upper Body Push", "Legs", "Conditioning"</li>
<li><strong>Add Exercises</strong> by tapping the <strong>+ ADD EXERCISE</strong> button</li>
</ol>

<h3>Step 4: Add Exercises to Days</h3>

<p>For each exercise, specify:</p>

<p><strong>Exercise Details:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Name</strong> – What exercise you're doing (e.g., "Bench Press", "Back Squat")</li>
<li><strong>Type</strong> – Strength or Conditioning</li>
<li><strong>Notes</strong> (optional) – Coaching cues or reminders</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>For Strength Exercises:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Number of Sets</strong> – How many sets (e.g., 4)</li>
<li><strong>Reps</strong> – Repetitions per set (e.g., 6)</li>
<li><strong>Weight</strong> – Starting weight in pounds or kg</li>
<li><strong>RPE</strong> (Rating of Perceived Exertion) – Scale of 1-10</li>
<li><strong>RIR</strong> (Reps in Reserve) – How many reps left in the tank</li>
<li><strong>Tempo</strong> – Movement speed (e.g., "3-1-1-0")</li>
<li><strong>Rest</strong> – Seconds between sets (e.g., 180)</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>For Conditioning Exercises:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Duration</strong> – Time in seconds</li>
<li><strong>Distance</strong> – Meters or miles</li>
<li><strong>Rounds</strong> – Number of rounds to complete</li>
<li><strong>Calories</strong> – Target calorie burn</li>
<li><strong>Target Pace</strong> – Desired pace (e.g., "2:00/500m")</li>
<li><strong>Effort Descriptor</strong> – Intensity level (e.g., "Easy", "Moderate", "Hard")</li>
<li><strong>Rest</strong> – Recovery time between intervals</li>
</ul>

<h3>Step 5: Save Your Block</h3>

<ol>
<li>Review all days and exercises</li>
<li>Tap <strong>SAVE</strong> in the top-right corner</li>
<li>Your block is now ready to run!</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>Pro Tip:</strong> Start with 3-4 days per week and 4-6 exercises per day for your first block.</p>

<h2 id="understanding-exercises">Understanding Exercises</h2>

<h3>Exercise Types</h3>

<p>The app supports two main exercise types:</p>

<h4>1. Strength Training</h4>
<p><strong>Characteristics:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Uses sets, reps, and weight</li>
<li>Focuses on resistance training</li>
<li>Examples: Squats, bench press, deadlifts, rows, curls</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>How to Log:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Enter the weight you lifted</li>
<li>Record actual reps performed</li>
<li>Mark RPE/RIR if tracking intensity</li>
<li>Check off each set as completed</li>
</ul>

<h4>2. Conditioning</h4>
<p><strong>Characteristics:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Uses time, distance, or calories</li>
<li>Focuses on cardiovascular fitness</li>
<li>Examples: Running, rowing, cycling, swimming, jump rope</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>How to Log:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Record time or distance covered</li>
<li>Note calories burned (if tracked)</li>
<li>Mark rounds completed for intervals</li>
<li>Set effort level or pace</li>
</ul>

<h3>Exercise Categories</h3>

<p>Exercises are organized by movement pattern:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Squat</strong> – Squat variations</li>
<li><strong>Hinge</strong> – Deadlift, Romanian deadlift, hip thrusts</li>
<li><strong>Press Horizontal</strong> – Bench press, push-ups, dips</li>
<li><strong>Press Vertical</strong> – Overhead press, push press</li>
<li><strong>Pull Horizontal</strong> – Rows, inverted rows</li>
<li><strong>Pull Vertical</strong> – Pull-ups, lat pulldowns</li>
<li><strong>Carry</strong> – Farmer's carries, suitcase carries</li>
<li><strong>Core</strong> – Planks, ab exercises</li>
<li><strong>Olympic</strong> – Cleans, snatches, jerks</li>
<li><strong>Conditioning</strong> – Cardio and metabolic work</li>
<li><strong>Mobility</strong> – Stretching and movement prep</li>
<li><strong>Other</strong> – Miscellaneous exercises</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="running-workout-sessions">Running Workout Sessions</h2>

<h3>Starting a Workout</h3>

<ol>
<li>Go to <strong>BLOCKS</strong> from the Home Screen</li>
<li>Find the block you want to run</li>
<li>Tap the <strong>RUN</strong> button</li>
<li>The app creates sessions for all weeks based on your templates</li>
</ol>

<h3>During Your Workout</h3>

<p><strong>Week &amp; Day Navigation:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Swipe left/right</strong> to change weeks</li>
<li><strong>Tap day tabs</strong> at the top to switch between workout days</li>
<li>The current week is highlighted at the top</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Logging Sets:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Each exercise shows <strong>expected values</strong> from your template</li>
<li>Tap a set to expand and log your actual performance</li>
<li>Use <strong>+ / -</strong> buttons to adjust weight, reps, time, distance, etc.</li>
<li>Check the <strong>✓</strong> box when you complete the set</li>
<li>The set turns green when marked complete</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Set Status Indicators:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Gray</strong> – Not started</li>
<li><strong>Green with checkmark</strong> – Completed</li>
<li><strong>Progress bar</strong> – Shows how many sets completed</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Auto-Save:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>The app automatically saves your progress as you go</li>
<li>You never lose data, even if you close the app mid-workout</li>
</ul>

<h3>Viewing the Workout Plan</h3>

<p><strong>Whiteboard Mode (Pro Feature):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Tap <strong>Whiteboard</strong> in the top-right during a workout</li>
<li>See a clean, printable view of your entire week</li>
<li>Great for referencing between sets or for coaches</li>
<li>Shows all exercises, sets, reps, and weights in an easy-to-read format</li>
</ul>

<h3>Finishing a Workout</h3>

<ol>
<li>Complete all the sets you plan to do</li>
<li>Tap <strong>Close Session</strong> in the top-left</li>
<li>The app saves all your logged data</li>
<li>Progress is recorded in your block history</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>Week Completion:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>When you finish all days in a week, you'll see a completion modal</li>
<li>Review your stats: sets completed, exercises done</li>
<li>Tap <strong>Continue to Next Week</strong> or <strong>Review Week</strong></li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Block Completion:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>When you finish the final week, you'll see a block completion celebration</li>
<li>The block is automatically archived to Block History</li>
<li>You can review it anytime from the History screen</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="ai-block-generation">AI Block Generation</h2>

<h3>What is AI Block Generation? (Pro Feature)</h3>

<p>The app can <strong>import training blocks from JSON files</strong> that are generated by AI or other tools. This allows coaches, programmers, or AI assistants to create structured blocks that you can import directly.</p>

<h3>How to Use AI Block Generator</h3>

<ol>
<li>From <strong>BLOCKS</strong> screen, tap <strong>AI BLOCK GENERATOR</strong></li>
<li>You have two options:
<ul>
<li><strong>Import from File</strong> – Select a <code>.json</code> file from your device</li>
<li><strong>Paste JSON</strong> – Copy and paste JSON directly into the app</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>

<h3>JSON Format Requirements</h3>

<p>The JSON must follow the Block structure:</p>
<ul>
<li>Block name, description, number of weeks</li>
<li>Day templates with exercises</li>
<li>Exercise details (sets, reps, progression)</li>
<li>Strength or conditioning parameters</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Example Use Cases:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Import programs from online coaches</li>
<li>Use ChatGPT or other AI to generate training blocks</li>
<li>Share blocks between devices via JSON files</li>
<li>Backup and transfer blocks</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Note:</strong> This feature is for <strong>importing</strong> blocks only. The app doesn't generate AI content itself—it imports blocks created externally.</p>

<h2 id="data-management">Data Management</h2>

<h3>Backing Up Your Data</h3>

<p><strong>Why Backup?</strong></p>
<p>Your workout data is stored locally on your device. Back it up to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Protect against data loss</li>
<li>Transfer to a new device</li>
<li>Keep an external copy for safety</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>How to Backup:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Go to <strong>DATA MANAGEMENT</strong> from Home Screen</li>
<li>Tap <strong>EXPORT ALL DATA</strong></li>
<li>Choose where to save the file (iCloud, Files app, etc.)</li>
<li>A JSON file is created with all your blocks and sessions</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>Backup Contents:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>All training blocks (active and archived)</li>
<li>All workout sessions and logged sets</li>
<li>Exercise library</li>
<li>Settings and preferences</li>
</ul>

<h3>Restoring Data</h3>

<p><strong>How to Restore:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Go to <strong>DATA MANAGEMENT</strong></li>
<li>Tap <strong>IMPORT DATA</strong></li>
<li>Select your backup JSON file</li>
<li>Confirm the import</li>
<li>All data is restored</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>Important Notes:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Importing <strong>replaces</strong> existing data</li>
<li>Always export current data before importing</li>
<li>Restore files must be valid JSON in the correct format</li>
</ul>

<h3>Starting Fresh</h3>

<p><strong>Clear All Data:</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Go to <strong>DATA MANAGEMENT</strong></li>
<li>Tap <strong>CLEAR ALL DATA</strong></li>
<li>Confirm the action (THIS CANNOT BE UNDONE)</li>
<li>Recommended: Export backup first</li>
</ol>

<p><strong>When to Clear Data:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Starting a new training phase completely fresh</li>
<li>Troubleshooting issues</li>
<li>Handing device to someone else</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="tips-best-practices">Tips &amp; Best Practices</h2>

<ol>

<ul>

<li>Focus on basic compound exercises</li>
<li>Use 3-4 sets of 5-8 reps for strength</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Learn the Interface</strong>
<ul>
<li>Run through a test workout to understand logging</li>
<li>Explore the Whiteboard view</li>
<li>Practice saving and editing blocks</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Be Consistent</strong>
<ul>
<li>Stick to your schedule</li>
<li>Log every set, even if you don't hit targets</li>
<li>Review your history weekly</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>

<h3>For Advanced Users</h3>

<ol>
<li><strong>Progressive Overload</strong>
<ul>
<li>Use the Delta Weight/Sets features</li>
<li>Plan deload weeks (week 4, 8, 12)</li>
<li>Track RPE/RIR for intensity management</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Block Programming</strong>
<ul>
<li>Create specialized blocks (strength, hypertrophy, peaking)</li>
<li>Use week templates for varied programming</li>
<li>Clone successful blocks and adjust for next cycle</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Superset &amp; Circuits</strong>
<ul>
<li>Group exercises by assigning Set Group IDs (in advanced mode)</li>
<li>Plan rest periods strategically</li>
<li>Use conditioning blocks for metabolic work</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>

<h3>General Tips</h3>

<p><strong>✅ DO:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Log every workout, even if incomplete</li>
<li>Review previous weeks before planning the next block</li>
<li>Use notes fields for coaching cues</li>
<li>Export data backups monthly</li>
<li>Screenshot your whiteboard for quick reference</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>❌ DON'T:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Skip logging—you lose valuable data</li>
<li>Delete blocks immediately after completion (archive first)</li>
<li>Forget to progress weight/volume across weeks</li>
<li>Ignore deload weeks—recovery is training</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="troubleshooting">Troubleshooting</h2>

<h3>Common Issues</h3>

<p><strong>Q: My workout isn't saving</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Check that you tapped the checkmark to complete sets</li>
<li>Ensure you have storage space on your device</li>
<li>Try closing and reopening the session</li>

<h2>Glossary</h2>

<dl>
<dt><strong>Block</strong></dt>
<dd>A complete training program spanning multiple weeks</dd>

<dt><strong>Day Template</strong></dt>
<dd>A workout day design that repeats each week</dd>

<dt><strong>Session</strong></dt>
<dd>A single workout instance with logged data</dd>

<dt><strong>Set</strong></dt>
<dd>One round of an exercise (e.g., 5 reps at 185 lbs)</dd>

<dt><strong>Progressive Overload</strong></dt>
<dd>Gradually increasing training stress (weight, volume, intensity)</dd>

<dt><strong>Deload</strong></dt>
<dd>Reduced training volume for recovery</dd>

<dt><strong>RPE (Rating of Perceived Exertion)</strong></dt>
<dd>Scale of 1-10, how hard the set felt</dd>

<dt><strong>RIR (Reps in Reserve)</strong></dt>
<dd>How many more reps you could have done</dd>

<dt><strong>Tempo</strong></dt>
<dd>Movement speed (eccentric-pause-concentric-pause in seconds)</dd>

<dt><strong>Superset</strong></dt>
<dd>Two exercises performed back-to-back with no rest</dd>

<dt><strong>Circuit</strong></dt>
<dd>Multiple exercises performed sequentially</dd>

<dt><strong>AMRAP</strong></dt>
<dd>As Many Reps/Rounds As Possible</dd>

<dt><strong>EMOM</strong></dt>
<dd>Every Minute On the Minute</dd>

<dt><strong>Periodization</strong></dt>
<dd>Systematic planning of training variables over time</dd>
</dl>
