## What This App Is For

Most fitness apps are designed for people who think about training. This one is for people who train.

Whether you lift, grapple, run, row, or just try to stay consistent through real life — this app tracks what matters without getting in your way.

---

## How It Works

### Structure Your Work

Build training blocks with multiple weeks, structured days, and progressive overload baked in. Plan once. Execute repeatedly.

### Track What You Actually Did

Log sets, reps, weight, time, distance, and effort. No judgment, no gamification, no artificial pressure. Just data.

### Review and Adjust

Look back at your history, spot patterns, and make smarter decisions about what to keep and what to change.

---

## Core Features

- **Training Blocks** – Structured programs spanning multiple weeks with built-in progression
- **Block Periodization** – Progressive overload, deload weeks, and systematic planning
- **Session Execution** – Track every set, rep, and metric during your workout
- **Exercise Types** – Strength training (sets/reps/weight) and conditioning (time/distance/rounds)
- **Block History** – Review every workout you've ever logged
- **Data Management** – Backup and restore your training data
- **Whiteboard View** (Pro) – Clean, printable workout display
- **AI Block Import** (Pro) – Import programs from coaches or AI-generated JSON

## The Point

You don't become better because of what you plan to do. You become better because of what you repeatedly do.

This app doesn't coach you or motivate you. It shows you the truth: did you show up, how often, and what changed.

**The rest is on you.**

---

## Built on Systems, Not Chance

The app is designed around proven training principles:

- **Progressive Overload** – Add weight or volume week-over-week
- **Block Periodization** – Plan multi-week cycles with clear phases
- **Tracking & Review** – Every session is captured and stored
- **Structured Execution** – Know exactly what to do when you walk into the gym
- **Consistency Over Intensity** – Showing up matters more than perfect sessions

No random workouts. No guesswork. Just deliberate progression.
