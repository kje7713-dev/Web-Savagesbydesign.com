# Quick Start Guide

**Get up and running in 10 minutes.**

This guide walks you through creating your first training block and running your first workout. Skip the theory. Just the steps you need to start tracking.

---

## Step 1: Launch the App

Open Savage By Design. You'll see four main buttons on the home screen:

- **BLOCKS** – Where you create and manage training programs
- **BLOCK HISTORY** – Your archived workouts
- **DATA MANAGEMENT** – Backup and restore
- **GO PRO** – Subscription features

**Tap BLOCKS to get started.**

---

## Step 2: Create Your First Block

On the Blocks screen, tap **CREATE NEW BLOCK** at the bottom.

### Set Block Details

1. **Name**: Give it a name like "4-Week Strength Program"
2. **Number of Weeks**: Start with 4 weeks
3. **Progression Type**: Choose "Weight" if you want to add weight each week, or "Volume" to add sets
4. **Delta Weight**: Enter 5 (adds 5 lbs per week to exercises)

---

## Step 3: Create Your First Day

You'll see tabs at the top: Day 1, Day 2, etc.

Tap **Day 1** and name it something like "Upper Body".

### Add Exercises

Tap **+ ADD EXERCISE** to add your first exercise.

**Example: Bench Press**
- **Name**: Bench Press
- **Type**: Strength
- **Sets**: 4
- **Reps**: 8
- **Weight**: 135 (your starting weight in lbs)
- **Rest**: 120 (seconds between sets)

Tap **Save** to add the exercise.

**Add 2-3 more exercises** using the same process:

- Overhead Press: 3 sets x 10 reps
- Barbell Rows: 4 sets x 8 reps
- Pull-ups: 3 sets x 6 reps

---

## Step 4: Add More Days (Optional)

Tap **Day 2** and repeat the process. Name it "Lower Body" and add exercises like:

- Back Squat: 4 sets x 8 reps
- Romanian Deadlift: 3 sets x 10 reps
- Leg Press: 3 sets x 12 reps

For your first block, 2-3 days is plenty.

---

## Step 5: Save Your Block

Once you've added all your days and exercises, tap **SAVE** in the top-right corner.

Your block is now created and ready to run!

---

## Step 6: Run Your First Workout

Back on the Blocks screen, you'll see your newly created block.

Tap **RUN** to start your first workout session.

### Navigate the Workout

- **Week selector** at the top shows which week you're on (Week 1)
- **Day tabs** let you switch between workout days
- **Exercise list** shows all your exercises for today

---

## Step 7: Log Your Sets

For each exercise, you'll see the planned sets.

**To log a set:**
1. Tap on the set to expand it
2. Adjust the weight, reps, or other values if needed using the **+ / -** buttons
3. Check the **✓** box when you complete the set
4. The set turns green to show it's logged

The app auto-saves as you go. You can close it anytime and come back later.

---

## Step 8: Finish Your Workout

When you're done:

1. Tap **Close Session** in the top-left
2. Your workout is saved automatically
3. All data goes into your Block History

---

## Step 9: Progress to Next Week

When you complete all days in Week 1, the app shows a completion screen.

Tap **Continue to Next Week** to move to Week 2.

**Progression happens automatically:**
- If you set Delta Weight to 5 lbs, all your exercises now show 5 lbs more
- If you set Delta Sets, you'll see an extra set added

---

## Step 10: Review Your History

From the home screen, tap **BLOCK HISTORY**.

You'll see all completed blocks. Tap **REVIEW** on any block to see:

- Every set you logged
- Weight progression over weeks
- Completion dates
- Notes you added

---

## Pro Tips for Getting Started

- **Start simple**: 3-4 days per week, 4-5 exercises per day
- **Be realistic**: Use weights you can complete with good form
- **Log everything**: Even if you don't hit your target, log what you did
- **Backup your data**: Go to DATA MANAGEMENT → EXPORT ALL DATA monthly
- **Check out Pro**: Whiteboard view is great for visualizing your week

## Common First-Time Questions

**Q: Can I edit a block after I create it?**

A: Yes. From the Blocks screen, tap the block, then tap Edit. Make your changes and save.

**Q: What if I miss a workout?**

A: No problem. The app doesn't force completion. Skip the day and come back whenever you can.

**Q: Can I add exercises mid-block?**

A: It's better to edit the block template before running it. Once running, the structure is set for that cycle.

**Q: How do I delete a block?**

A: Swipe left on the block in the Blocks screen, then tap Delete.

**Q: What's the difference between Blocks and Block History?**

A: BLOCKS shows active training programs. BLOCK HISTORY shows completed/archived programs.
