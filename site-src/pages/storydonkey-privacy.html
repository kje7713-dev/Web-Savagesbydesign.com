StoryDonkey Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how Savages By Design handles information for StoryDonkey, an app for developing stories from an initial idea through planning, writing, continuity, and revision. “We,” “us,” and “our” mean Savages By Design. “StoryDonkey” means the app, website, and related services.

Information we collect

  • Interest signups: If you join the StoryDonkey interest list, we collect your email address and selected story arc. These details are sent to our operator by email so we can process beta interest.
  • Support messages: If you contact support, we receive the information you include in your message.
  • Story and project content: When the app is available, StoryDonkey may process the projects, characters, outlines, scenes, and other content you choose to enter or upload so the service can provide its features.
  • Technical information: Our hosting and service providers may process ordinary technical information needed to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the website or app.

How we use information

  • To maintain the interest list and contact you about StoryDonkey availability or important service information.
  • To provide, operate, secure, maintain, and improve StoryDonkey.
  • To respond to support requests and prevent abuse, fraud, or security problems.
  • To comply with law and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your story content to claim ownership of your work.

Story content and third parties

StoryDonkey may rely on hosting, email, analytics, payment, authentication, storage, and model or infrastructure providers. Those providers may process information only as needed to provide services to us, comply with law, or protect their systems. The specific providers may change as the service develops.

You should not submit information that you are not authorized to share. You are responsible for reviewing the settings and terms of any third-party service you choose to connect or use.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including providing the service, maintaining business and security records, resolving disputes, and complying with legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we control, subject to legal and operational limits. You may unsubscribe from non-essential email communications by using the unsubscribe option where provided or contacting support.

Apple App Store disclosure

If you download StoryDonkey from Apple’s App Store, Apple may process purchase, subscription, device, and account information under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. Apple’s privacy policy is available at apple.com/legal/privacy. We do not receive your payment card details from Apple.

Children

StoryDonkey is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as StoryDonkey changes. We will post the revised policy at this URL and update the date above.
